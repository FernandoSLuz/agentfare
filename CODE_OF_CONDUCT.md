# Code of conduct

AgentFare follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. This is the short form. The full text is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that help keep the community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, and not only for ourselves.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission. This includes pasting someone's agent logs or prompts.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when they do.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all the project's spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Fernando Silva da Luz ([@FernandoSLuz](https://github.com/FernandoSLuz)), through GitHub. A report that can be public can be an issue. A report that must stay private can go through the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/FernandoSLuz/agentfare/security), where Report a vulnerability opens a private advisory that only you and the maintainer can read. Say in it that it is a conduct report.

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer will respect the privacy and security of the person who reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer follows these steps when deciding the consequences of an action that breaks this Code of Conduct.

1. Correction. For inappropriate language or behavior that is judged unprofessional or unwelcome. The consequence is a private, written explanation of what was wrong and why, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. Warning. For a violation through a single incident or a series of actions. The consequence is a warning, with no interaction with the people involved for a set period, including with the people who enforce this code. Breaking the terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. Temporary ban. For a serious violation, or sustained inappropriate behavior. The consequence is a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the project for a set period. Breaking the terms may lead to a permanent ban.
4. Permanent ban. For a pattern of violations, sustained harassment of a person, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people. The consequence is a permanent ban from any public interaction with the project.
